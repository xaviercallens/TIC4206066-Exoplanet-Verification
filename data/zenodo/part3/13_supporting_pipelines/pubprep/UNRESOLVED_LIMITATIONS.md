| L1 | **No high-resolution imaging.** A bound companion within ~1″ (≲ 36 au) is not excluded by any public data. For the 11.13 d signal it is the dominant false-positive channel (TRICERATOPS STP ≈ 0.13–0.20). For the 3.18 d signal a *circular-orbit* host must be ≥ 0.54 M☉ (transit density) and is excluded by the Gaia XP overluminosity; eccentric configurations (e ≳ 0.6 at favourable ω) and lower-mass hosts on such orbits remain. | both (B strongly) | needs speckle/AO | no |

The Note calls it a low-significance candidate; TESS Sector 110 (3 predicted transits, ±3 min) is the test. | B | only ~21 d of S6 data, S98 scattered light | no (S110, Nov 2026) |

| L5 | **A's localisation is at 2σ from the target** in the combined S32 + S98 clean data ((+8 ± 5, −8 ± 7)″; χ² = 5.3 for 2 dof, p ≈ 0.07) and the S98 x-offset differs between the two independent implementations by ~1σ (this work +1.05, audit +0.07). The nearest Gaia neighbour (11″, T = 18.2) would need a ≥ 100 % eclipse to produce a 500 ppm dip (89 % against the lower difference-image amplitude of the S32 TPF alone); the neighbour-injection test disfavours it only at ~1.5σ in S32 and excludes it in S98 (> 100 %).
